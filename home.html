what is nhd

nhd is A year-long educational program and competition for students in grades 4–12 to conduct historical research and present their findings through papers, websites, exhibits, performances, or documentaries. Students choose a historical topic related to an annual theme, research it using primary and secondary sources, and then create a project to present their conclusions. Projects are entered into local, state, and national competitions where they are judged by professional historians and educators.
