Annotated bibliography

  1. “The Stamp Act of 1765.” Stamp Act of 1765, Aug. 2017, p. 1. EBSCOhost Link

    This is a resource we used, as it covers how the Stamp Act happened and what it did.

  2. “Resolutions of the Stamp Act Congress.” Resolutions of the Stamp Act Congress, Aug. 2017, p. 1. EBSCOhost Link

    Shows the response to the Stamp Act, helping with our project’s theme on reactions.

  3. “Stamp Act.” Funk & Wagnalls New World Encyclopedia, Jan. 2018, p. 1. EBSCOhost Link

    Explains the Stamp Act in detail; useful for describing what the act did.

  4. Orihel, Michelle. “Community without Consent: New Perspectives on the Stamp Act.” Early American Literature, vol. 53, no. 1, Jan. 2018, pp. 270–74. Link

    Explains public response to the Stamp Act, useful for response and reaction analysis.

  5. Sherwood, Martha A. “Stamp Act.” Salem Press Encyclopedia, Apr. 2023. EBSCOhost Link

    Summary of the Stamp Act events and key figures, helpful for explaining the act.

    6. Primary Sources

  6. “Petition of London Merchants against the Stamp Act.” Petition of London Merchants Against the Stamp Act, Aug. 2017, p. 1. EBSCOhost Link

    Text of a petition by London merchants requesting repeal; highlights trade concerns and tax difficulties.

  7. “America’s Taxes, Then and Now.” Congressional Digest, vol. 30, no. 10, Oct. 1951, pp. 229–35. EBSCOhost Link

    Discusses differences in taxation laws since independence; shows varying opinions and debates.

  8. “Virginia Stamp Act Resolutions.” Virginia Stamp Act Resolutions, Aug. 2017, p. 1. EBSCOhost Link

    Text of Virginia resolutions, showing the colony’s response to England’s Stamp Act.

  9. “The Continental Congress and the Articles of Confederation.” Congressional Digest, vol. 18, no. 4, Apr. 1939, pp. 108–09. EBSCOhost Link

    Rejection of Franklin's proposal; details the Stamp Act Congress meetings and decisions.

  10. “Northampton County Resolutions on the Stamp Act.” Northampton County Resolutions on the Stamp Act, Aug. 2017, p. 1. EBSCOhost Link

    Resolutions by Northampton County, VA, regarding the Stamp Act; shows the act’s unbinding nature.
