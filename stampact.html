what was the stamp act ?

The Stamp Act was a 1765 British law that imposed a direct tax on the American colonies for the first time, requiring that a stamp be purchased and affixed to all legal documents, newspapers, playing cards, and other paper goods

who did this affect ?

American colonists in the 13 colonies, printers, publishers, lawyers, customers,ect

how and when did it start ?

In 1764, the British Parliament passed a new law called the Stamp Act, which imposed a tax on all paper products. Britain created this tax as a result of a very taxing war known as the “French and Indian War.”
